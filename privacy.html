Privacy policy

Last updated 1 November 2026. Downburst: Download Manager is made by Vishwadeep Khare.

What we collect

Nothing. Downburst has no servers, no accounts and no analytics, and the developer receives no data from the app or its plug-in.

What stays on your Mac

Your download list and settings are stored on your Mac. For a download that isn't finished, this includes the site cookies needed to continue it. They are removed from the saved list when it finishes.

The Safari plug-in

Safari warns that the plug-in can read the pages you visit, because it works on all websites. The plug-in uses that access to see the download links you click. When you send a download to the app, it passes along the link, the page it came from and that site's cookies, so downloads that need you to be signed in can work.

The plug-in doesn't read what you type and doesn't send anything to the developer. You can turn it off in Safari at any time.

Who the app talks to

Downburst connects only to the sites hosting the files you download. Those sites receive what any download request sends, including your IP address and the cookies and browser details for that site. Cookies aren't sent on to a different site if a download redirects. Those sites' own privacy policies apply.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, this page will be updated. Questions: open a request.
