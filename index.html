Downburst support

Downburst: Download Manager is a Mac app for faster, resumable downloads.

Get help

Open a support request. It needs a free GitHub account, and requests are public, so don't include private details or links you need kept secret.

Common questions

Safari warns that the plug-in can read pages. Why? Safari shows this for any plug-in that works on all websites. Downburst's plug-in needs that access to see which link you click. See the privacy policy.

A download link wasn't caught in Safari. Safari catches links that lead directly to a file. For any other link, right-click it and choose Download with Downburst, or paste the link into the app with File > New Download.

Why isn't my download faster? Speed depends on the server and your connection. Downburst helps most on servers that limit each connection.

Where are my files? In your Downloads folder.
